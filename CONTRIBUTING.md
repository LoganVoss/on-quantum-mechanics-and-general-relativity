# Contributing a replication or correction

Please include the exact repository commit, environment, command, and raw output for a replication. For a mathematical correction, identify the proposition, its assumptions, and a derivation or counterexample. For a numerical issue, retain initial data, grid and timestep, boundary treatment, error measure, and convergence results.

Keep new execution output separate from archived evidence. Do not replace a recorded failure with a passing receipt. Distinguish exact identities, theorem statements, finite numerical observations, and physical interpretations. A new claim should include the smallest check that could falsify it.

The repository's issue tracker is the public venue for reports. A passing build checks the encoded propositions and does not confer peer-review status on the complete physical interpretation.
