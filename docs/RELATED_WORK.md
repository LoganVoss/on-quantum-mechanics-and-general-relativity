# Related work and contribution

The mathematical ingredients have a substantial history. This edition presents the conditional calibration theorem, an explicit quantum-to-orbit dictionary, and a reproducible evidence package. It makes additional consequences explicit in the same conventions. A bounded literature review does not establish worldwide priority.

| Primary source | Relationship to this bundle |
|---|---|
| [Banchi, Braunstein & Pirandola, *Quantum fidelity for arbitrary Gaussian states* (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01941v1) | General Gaussian fidelity and information geometry. The finite-distance identity here is a direct one-mode pure-state specialization with squared-fidelity conventions made explicit. |
| [Garcia-Chung, *On the geometric phase for Gaussian states* (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14204v1) | Gaussian geometric phases. This edition derives the Berry curvature in its $(u,v)$ coordinates and identifies the model's invariant odd boundary term. |
| [Seshadri, Lakshmibala & Balakrishnan, *Geometric phases for generalized squeezed coherent states* (1997; arXiv 1999)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9905101v1) | Earlier squeezed-state geometric-phase results; geometric phase is established quantum mechanics. |
| [Cruz, Miković & Navarro-Salas, *Free Field Realization of Cylindrically Symmetric Einstein Gravity* (1998)](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9802067v3) | Cylindrical gravity and its reduced field description. The gravitational reduction is prior work, not a new discovery claimed by this repository. |
| [Grumiller, Kummer & Vassilevich, *Dilaton Gravity in Two Dimensions* (2002; revised 2008)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0204253v9) | Background on the metric–dilaton action and frame choices. |
| [Hoare, Levine & Tseytlin, *Sigma models with local couplings* (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.01112v3) | Section 1 explicitly discusses harmonic local weights and two-Killing reductions; the present Lax derivation belongs to that established setting. |
| [Nicolai, Korotkin & Samtleben, *Integrable Classical and Quantum Gravity* (1996)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9612065v1) | Broader precedent for integrability in reduced gravity. |
| [Pravda et al., *Exact solutions to quadratic gravity* (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02646v3) | Explains why surviving vacuum solutions alone do not eliminate higher-curvature dynamics. |
| [Niedermaier, *Dimensionally reduced gravity theories are asymptotically safe* (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0304117v1) | A concrete renormalization direction for reduced models. Its results do not establish a continuum limit for the regulator in this archive. |

The original v2.4 package provides the all-field variation, connected-domain derivative closure, physical-model witnesses, odd-coupling selection, Lax necessity/sufficiency calculation, and corrected nonlinear evolutions. This edition independently rebuilds and reproduces those artifacts, adds the finite-distance/Berry dictionary, completes the residual bounds for $U,W$ and integrates them, and supplies an exact nonlinear numerical oracle. The inherited sharp $V,F''$ bounds are credited in the quantitative extension.

Sources were checked against primary paper records or full texts on November 13, 2026. Third-party papers are linked, not redistributed. Historical Voss documents are preserved with their original version labels and evidence status in [provenance](PROVENANCE.md).
