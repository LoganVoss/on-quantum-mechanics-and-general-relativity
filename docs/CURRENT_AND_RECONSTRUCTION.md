# Quantum sources and the reconstruction of geometry

The reciprocal coupling introduces a real consistency condition. In the vacuum one-Killing model, the scalar twist determines a connection curvature through $F=-\lambda^{-2}*d\chi$. A quantum source for $\chi$ generally makes this two-form nonclosed. It cannot then equal $dA$.

The remedy is a **current-corrected twist relation**, derived from the same local action as the quantum evolution. The correction includes a cross term and a current-squared term in the connection description. Keeping those terms gives an exact local scalar–connection equivalence for a smooth current. The construction is a variational state-fluid extension of the selected three-dimensional theory; its clock and matter assumptions are stated explicitly below.

## Fields, clock, and quantum Hamiltonian

Use the [one-Killing dictionary](ONE_KILLING_EXTENSION.md), with an arbitrary $2+1$ Lorentzian metric $h$, $\lambda>0$, and real twist $\chi$. Let

```math
q^A=(\lambda,\chi),
\qquad
G=\frac{d\lambda^2+d\chi^2}{\lambda^2},
\qquad
M=\frac1\lambda
\begin{pmatrix}1&\chi\\\chi&\chi^2+\lambda^2\end{pmatrix}.
```

An independent normalized probe state $|\Psi\rangle$ belongs to a calibrated oscillator Hilbert space with $[\widehat Q,\widehat P]=i$. It is an additional degree of freedom; it is not counted a second time as the geometric field $M$. The selected positive centered quadratic Hamiltonian is

```math
\widehat H_M
=\frac{\hbar\nu}{2}\widehat R^{\mathsf T}M^{-1}\widehat R
=\frac{\hbar\nu}{2\lambda}
\left[(\widehat P-\chi\widehat Q)^2+\lambda^2\widehat Q^2\right],
\qquad \nu>0,
```

with symmetric ordering understood. Its selection assumptions and observable consequences are in [Dynamical coupling](DYNAMICAL_COUPLING.md). A constant ground-energy subtraction changes none of the shape sources and can be absorbed into the rest-energy convention below.

The time used by this Hamiltonian is the proper flow time of **the three-dimensional Einstein-frame metric $h$**. This is an explicit physical calibration. It must not be silently replaced by four-dimensional proper time.

We assume a smooth local family of normalized states with the regularity needed for the displayed derivatives and finite operator expectations; centered Gaussian Schwartz states provide a concrete domain. This section treats the quantum state as a variational matter variable coupled to classical geometry and fluid variables. It does not claim a quantized gravitational continuum or a general measurement theory for classical–quantum hybrids.

## A smooth current generated by a common action

Introduce a future timelike vector density $j^a$, a scalar velocity potential $\vartheta$, and

```math
n=\frac{\sqrt{-h_{ab}j^aj^b}}{\sqrt{-h}},
\qquad
u^a=\frac{j^a}{\sqrt{-h}\,n},
\qquad h_{ab}u^au^b=-1.
```

The number density is $n$. Let $m_0$ be a fixed nonnegative rest-energy parameter and define

```math
\varepsilon(q,\Psi)=m_0+\langle\Psi|\widehat H_M|\Psi\rangle.
```

One concrete smooth-current matter action is

```math
S_{\mathrm m}
=\int d^3x\left[
j^a\left(\partial_a\vartheta
+i\hbar\langle\Psi|\partial_a\Psi\rangle\right)
-\sqrt{-h}\,n\varepsilon
\right].
```

State normalization is imposed in the variation, equivalently by a multiplier. The Berry term is real on normalized states. A phase change $\Psi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\Psi$ is compensated by $\vartheta\mapsto\vartheta+\hbar\alpha$.

A single velocity potential defines a restricted canonical-velocity subclass. Adding Clebsch terms $\sum_I\beta_I\partial_a\alpha^I$ inside the current bracket admits more general local fluid flows, with the labels advected by the current. This is standard fluid-action structure, rather than a newly inferred matter architecture. [Brown, *Action functionals for relativistic perfect fluids* (1993)](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9304026v1).

The complete action is

```math
S=S_{\mathrm{geom}}+S_{\mathrm m},
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{geom}}
=f\int\sqrt{-h}\left[
R[h]-\frac12G_{AB}\nabla q^A\cdot\nabla q^B
\right]d^3x,
\qquad f>0.
```

The selected vacuum theory is recovered when the probe current is absent. Choosing this smooth state-fluid architecture, its rest energy, and its clock is additional physical input. The selection of $\widehat H_M$ fixes the shape dependence within its declared probe class; it does not select every possible matter sector.

### Reciprocal equations and stress

Variation of $\vartheta$ gives

```math
\partial_a j^a=0
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\nabla_a(nu^a)=0.
```

State variation gives the Schrödinger equation along the flow, up to a removable normalization/phase multiplier:

```math
i\hbar u^a\partial_a|\Psi\rangle=\widehat H_M|\Psi\rangle.
```

For the single-potential subclass, variation of $j^a$ gives

```math
\partial_a\vartheta+i\hbar\langle\Psi|\partial_a\Psi\rangle
+\varepsilon u_a=0.
```

The Clebsch terms add their corresponding canonical-velocity contributions. Holding the vector density fixed during inverse-metric variation gives

```math
\delta(\sqrt{-h}\,n)
=\frac12\sqrt{-h}\,n u_a u_b\,\delta h^{ab},
\qquad
T^{\mathrm m}_{ab}=n\varepsilon u_a u_b.
```

Define $S_{ab}=G_{AB}\partial_aq^A\partial_bq^B$, $X=h^{ab}S_{ab}$, and $\widehat H_A=\partial\widehat H_M/\partial q^A$, with the state held fixed in this partial derivative. The common action gives

```math
2f\,\mathrm{Einstein}_{ab}
=f\left(S_{ab}-\frac12h_{ab}X\right)+T^{\mathrm m}_{ab},
```

```math
f\tau^A(q)=nG^{AB}\langle\widehat H_B\rangle,
```

where

```math
\tau^\lambda
=\Box\lambda-\frac{(\nabla\lambda)^2-(\nabla\chi)^2}{\lambda},
\qquad
\tau^\chi
=\Box\chi-\frac{2\nabla\lambda\cdot\nabla\chi}{\lambda}.
```

These equations provide both directions of influence: geometry enters the quantum Hamiltonian, and the quantum expectations source geometry.

The exchange is constrained by the action's diffeomorphism identity. On the matter equations,

```math
\nabla_aT^{\mathrm m\,a}{}_{b}
=-n\langle\widehat H_A\rangle\partial_bq^A.
```

The divergence of the scalar stress is the opposite quantity by its sourced wave-map equation. The combined source therefore satisfies the Einstein Bianchi identity. Along the flow,

```math
u^a\partial_a\varepsilon
=\langle\widehat H_A\rangle u^a\partial_aq^A,
```

and the projected force equation is

```math
\varepsilon u^a\nabla_a u_b
=-(\delta_b{}^a+u_bu^a)
\langle\widehat H_A\rangle\partial_aq^A.
```

These are local variational and conservation identities. They do not prove global smooth evolution, prevent fluid caustics, or establish a quantum continuum.

## Why the vacuum twist relation fails with a source

The sourced twist equation is

```math
f\nabla_a(\lambda^{-2}\nabla^a\chi)
=n\langle\widehat H_\chi\rangle.
```

If the vacuum formula were retained unchanged,

```math
F_{\mathrm{vac}}=-\lambda^{-2}*d\chi,
```

it would give

```math
dF_{\mathrm{vac}}
=-\frac n f\langle\widehat H_\chi\rangle\,\mathrm{vol}_h.
```

A nonzero right-hand side contradicts $F_{\mathrm{vac}}=dA$.

This is not merely a possible corner case. For a centered independent probe with $N=2\Sigma$, the excess energy is

```math
E_{\mathrm{ex}}
=\frac{\hbar\nu}{4}\left[\mathrm{Tr}(M^{-1}N)-2\right],
\qquad
\partial_\chi E_{\mathrm{ex}}
=\frac{\hbar\nu}{2\lambda}(\chi N_{11}-N_{12}).
```

At $M=I$ and

```math
N=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
```

the probe covariance is positive and unimodular, while $\partial_\chi E_{\mathrm{ex}}=-\hbar\nu/2$. The unchanged vacuum reconstruction fails for this admissible state. The negative control is retained in the exact checker.

## The quantum shear exposes the conserved current

Set

```math
U_\chi=\exp\left(i\chi\widehat Q^2/2\right),
\qquad |\Psi\rangle=U_\chi|\widetilde\Psi\rangle.
```

Then

```math
\widehat H_M=U_\chi\widehat H_0(\lambda)U_\chi^\dagger,
\qquad
\widehat H_0(\lambda)
=\frac{\hbar\nu}{2\lambda}
\left(\widehat P^2+\lambda^2\widehat Q^2\right).
```

The transformed Hamiltonian expectation has no explicit $\chi$ dependence. The Berry term transforms exactly as

```math
i\hbar\langle\Psi|\partial_a\Psi\rangle
=i\hbar\langle\widetilde\Psi|\partial_a\widetilde\Psi\rangle
-\hbar\left\langle\frac{\widehat Q^2}{2}\right\rangle\partial_a\chi.
```

The expectation of $\widehat Q^2$ is unchanged by this shear. Define the smooth vector current

```math
J^a=\hbar n u^a\left\langle\frac{\widehat Q^2}{2}\right\rangle
=\frac{\hbar j^a}{\sqrt{-h}}
\left\langle\frac{\widehat Q^2}{2}\right\rangle.
```

In the transformed variables, the complete $\chi$ dependence is

```math
\mathcal L_\chi
=-\frac{f}{2\lambda^2}(d\chi)^2-J\cdot d\chi.
```

Its equation conserves the total one-form current

```math
C=f\lambda^{-2}d\chi+J,
\qquad d(*C)=0.
```

Consequently,

```math
\boxed{
F=-\frac1f*C,\qquad dF=0,
\qquad
d\chi=\lambda^2\left(*F-\frac Jf\right).}
```

Here $J$ in a form expression denotes the metric-lowered one-form. On a contractible patch, the closed $F$ has a local potential $A$. The probe current corrects the twist relation rather than violating the connection's Bianchi identity.

For a one-probe mechanical reduction, the same symmetry gives a useful conserved quantity:

```math
\boxed{p_\chi-\hbar\left\langle\frac{\widehat Q^2}{2}\right\rangle
=\mathrm{constant}.}
```

Indeed, $\partial_\chi\langle\widehat H_M\rangle=-\hbar\,d\langle\widehat Q^2/2\rangle/dt$ by the Heisenberg equation, while the shape momentum obeys $\dot p_\chi=-\partial_\chi\langle\widehat H_M\rangle$. This supplies a concrete check on reciprocal transfer.

## Exact first-order dualization

Introduce an independent one-form $v$, keep $F=dA$, and write

```math
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{master}}
=-\frac f{2\lambda^2}v_av^a-J^av_a
+f v_a(*F)^a.
```

The remaining gravitational and matter terms are unchanged. In the shear variables, $J$ depends on $h,j,\widetilde\Psi$ but not on $v,\chi$, or $A$.

Varying $A$ gives $dv=0$, so locally $v=d\chi$. The final master term then contributes only a boundary term, reproducing the scalar formulation.

Varying $v$ instead gives

```math
v=\lambda^2\left(*F-\frac Jf\right).
```

Eliminating $v$ yields

```math
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{dual}}
=-\frac{f\lambda^2}{4}F_{ab}F^{ab}
-\lambda^2J\cdot *F
+\frac{\lambda^2}{2f}J_aJ^a.}
```

Equivalently, with the square denoting two-form contraction,

```math
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{dual}}
=-\frac{f\lambda^2}{4}
\left(F+\frac{*J}{f}\right)_{ab}
\left(F+\frac{*J}{f}\right)^{ab}.
```

The cross term and the current-squared contact term are fixed by the Legendre transformation. Dropping either would change the scalar equations and their reciprocal sources. The displayed algebra uses $*^2=-1$ and was checked component by component in a local $(-,+,+)$ frame.

$J$ has metric and state dependence. That dependence must be retained when varying the dual action; it is held fixed only for the algebraic elimination of $v$. With consistent variations, the master action gives the same local equations in either description.

For ideal point-worldline delta currents, $J^2$ is undefined without a regulator. This derivation uses a smooth current or an explicitly finite regulator. It is not a point-particle renormalization theorem.

## What the reconstruction establishes

The local connection can now be reconstructed consistently, and inserted into

```math
g_4=\lambda^{-1}h+\lambda(dy+A)^2.
```

The resulting four-metric is generally sourced rather than vacuum. The reduced action is a specified three-dimensional Einstein–matter extension with a current-corrected connection sector. These calculations do not identify it automatically with an ordinary four-dimensional minimally coupled matter theory.

The clock distinction makes this limitation concrete. For a horizontal four-dimensional trajectory, $dy+A_a dx^a=0$, the dictionary gives

```math
d\tau_4=\lambda^{-1/2}ds_3.
```

A fixed four-dimensional proper-time oscillator gap would therefore appear $\lambda^{-1/2}$-dependent when expressed in the three-dimensional proper time $s_3$. The constant target-equivariant gap used here is calibrated to $h$. A physical four-dimensional matter uplift must explain its clock and source identification; scalar–connection duality alone does not provide that explanation.
