# Next tests that can change the conclusion

The exact bridge is useful because it narrows the next questions. Each route below has a concrete success condition.

## 1. Test the quantum geometry with prepared states

Prepare centered squeezed one-mode Gaussian states with measured covariance and purity. Compare transition probabilities to $\operatorname{sech}(d_G/2)$ and closed-path geometric phases to minus one quarter of the oriented hyperbolic area. Account for displacement, mixedness, loss, phase reference, and covariance uncertainty. This tests the quantum-state geometry and calibration. It does not by itself test the identification of that shape with a spacetime orbit metric.

The oscillator vacuum/first-excited-state counterexample in this bundle is the essential negative control: equal normalized covariance shape is insufficient outside the saturated family.

## 2. Turn calibration error into coefficient intervals

Use the explicit five-residual inversion on a fixed compact positive area interval. Preserve the chart units and residual normalization. Propagate a justified simultaneous error envelope through the sharp bounds and the logarithmic integration estimates. The success condition is an interval for each coefficient function supported by those residual bounds, rather than a fit to a preselected affine ansatz.

This requires independent access to the calibrated field-equation residuals or a justified measurement-to-residual model. Merely evaluating equations selected by the theorem does not create empirical evidence for the theorem's premises.

## 3. Distinguish theories beyond the derivative class

Flat and Ricci-flat reference backgrounds leave many curvature-squared terms invisible. Use a calibrated sourced response or a curved configuration that differentiates the proposed completion. The v2.4 Levi-Civita calculation already excludes one specified $\Lambda(\rho)\mathcal N^2$ deformation under its assumptions. A broader theorem must inventory the enlarged operator class and prove that its proposed tests distinguish all remaining coefficients.

## 4. Move beyond the present numerical benchmark

Retain the exact nonlinear Bessel/geodesic oracle as a regression test. Next, examine longer evolutions and independently varied two-profile data with separate spatial, temporal, and outer-boundary refinement. Success requires resolved errors and balance accounting across the declared family. A general global-existence or stability claim requires a PDE argument; additional finite simulations alone cannot supply it.

## 5. Quantize the selected theory with controlled limits

The recovered archive includes a positive regulated Hamiltonian and a conditional opening-operator reconstruction argument. The next obligation is to construct the interacting radial continuum, control renormalization and spectral loss, and demonstrate the relevant physical constraints and observable algebra. [Niedermaier's reduced-gravity program](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0304117v1) is relevant prior work, not a substitute for those proofs in this model.

## 6. Extend the geometric sector

Allow mixed base–orbit connections, additional modes, or fewer Killing symmetries. Reclassify the invariant action and repeat the calibration argument rather than assuming the same coefficient selection. A successful extension must state which new fields and physical calibrations are required, and reproduce the present result in the two-symmetry limit.
